## Current evidence level

The completed local calculation supports a finite-size, field-free
orientational-memory regime generated by pair interactions on a caged
disordered graph. It does not establish a thermodynamic glass transition.
Every numerical value quoted in the Letter is reconstructed by the claim
audit, which currently passes 110 of 110 checks.

The two-pulse calculation additionally establishes a signed sequence readout
after both fields are removed. At \(N=144\) and \(h=8k_{\rm B}T\), the
quarter-width contested support retains
\(M_{\rm ord}=0.5985\pm0.0015\), the separated-support control gives
\(0.0265\pm0.0104\), and full support gives \(0.667\pm0.022\). The
\(N=256\) endpoint is \(0.5781\pm0.0030\) against
\(0.0015\pm0.0010\) for separated supports. A coupled double-well control
shows why the pulse commutator alone cannot define memory: its full-support
bracket is largest where relaxation erases the AB/BA difference. The retained
observable is therefore defined after quotienting the driven states by the
field-free relaxation map.

The numerical and provenance gates are separate. The cluster-generated
`activated_memory_scan.jsonl` shards are now installed, the audit's
`raw_reproduces_derived_report` gate passes, and Fig. 4 has been rebuilt from
them with no change to any published value (`max_relative_change` 0.0). The
figure reports the endpoint retained overlap at the common observation time.
Identical raw-row duplicates are removed by stable record key; conflicting
duplicates abort the rebuild.

The positional-disorder retention values also pass their numerical checks,
but the local summary records that the underlying `N=576` and `N=1024`
write--release trajectories have not yet been installed. Consequently the
publication-level provenance gate is false even though the quantitative audit
passes.

## Resolved: Fig. 4(b) window censoring

Panel (b) previously reported the finite-window integrated overlap
`A_Q = int_0^{T_obs} max[Q(t),0] dt / |Q(0)|`. Because `Q(t) <= Q(0)`, the
quantity `D_r A_Q` is bounded above by the observation window (625), so the
physical branch saturated against that ceiling at large coupling (74% of it at
lambda = 1.4) while the `g = 0` branch continued to grow. The enhancement ratio
therefore peaked near lambda = 1.05 and fell afterwards for an instrumental
reason rather than a physical one.

The panel now reports the endpoint retained overlap `Q(T_obs)`, which has no
such ceiling. It rises monotonically from 0.001 to 0.691 across the scanned
range, while the matched `g = 0` control is consistent with zero at every
coupling. Retention is stated as a separation from the null control in units of
the combined standard error (3.4 sigma at lambda = 0.60, rising to 35-45 sigma
for lambda >= 0.90) rather than as a ratio: recomputing the old ratio on the
endpoint measure returns -169 and -55, sign-flipped by a denominator consistent
with zero, which is what made it unstable.

The two audit checks that froze the old ratios were replaced by separation
checks, and the provenance gate now recomputes the endpoint measure from the
raw shards. The audit passes 110 of 110 with `raw_reproduces_derived_report`
true.

## Rebuild Fig. 4

The eight-coupling retention scan is a separate job. It shards the five
quenched graphs over whatever CUDA devices are visible, so the same command
works on one RTX 3090 and on a four-GPU node:

    nohup bash scripts/run_rotating_colloids_activated_memory_prl_gpu.sh \
      > rotating_colloids_activated_memory_gpu.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &

It writes `activated_memory_scan.jsonl` shards under
`discoveries/theory_experiment_interface/rotating_colloids_hyperion/rotating_colloids_activated_memory_prl_gpu/`
and then rebuilds `fig4_activated_memory_results.pdf` and its report. The scan
is resumable: rerunning skips completed graph/coupling points.

The plot script compares the new report against the previous one and writes
`activated_memory_report_delta.json`. A nonzero `max_relative_change` means
the numbers quoted in the Letter and the frozen expectations in
`scripts/audit_rotating_colloids_capillary_prl.py` must be updated together.
Keep a copy of the current `activated_memory_figure_report.json` before the
rerun so the comparison is meaningful.

Once the raw activated-memory shards and disorder-protocol directories exist,
the audit's provenance block reports complete raw support and the deposit can
be completed:

    python -B scripts/build_rotating_colloids_release.py \
      --root "$PWD" \
      --disorder-retention-input /path/to/disorder_protocol_runs \
      --output-dir "$PWD/release/zenodo_geometry_encoded_orientational_memory" \
      --clean
